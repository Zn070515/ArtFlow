# ArtFlow Commercial Licensing

This document describes how to request a commercial license. It is not a
commercial license agreement, quotation, invoice, warranty, or support contract.
Only a separate written agreement signed by the actual ArtFlow copyright
holder(s) can grant commercial rights beyond the applicable PolyForm license.

## When to ask

Ask for a commercial license before relying on ArtFlow for a use that is not
permitted by the selected PolyForm license. Typical examples include:

- distributing ArtFlow or a modified version as part of a paid product;
- operating a paid or otherwise commercial hosted/SaaS service based on ArtFlow;
- reselling, sublicensing, or bundling the software for customers;
- delivering a paid commercial deployment or customization where the software
  rights themselves are part of the commercial offering; or
- granting a customer rights that the selected PolyForm license does not grant.

These examples are not an interpretation of the standard licenses. Read the
full text in `licenses/` for the actual free-license terms and obtain legal
advice for a particular business model.

## Information to include in an inquiry

Describe the proposed:

- legal entity and contact person;
- product, service, or deployment and whether it is hosted or distributed;
- number of installations, tenants, seats, customers, or other relevant limits;
- modified components and any redistribution or sublicensing;
- requested term, territory, update rights, and support/maintenance scope; and
- attribution, audit, security, and termination requirements.

Commercial terms, pricing, support, upgrades, and deployment rights must be
agreed in writing. Contact the repository owner or the official ArtFlow
distribution channel to identify the current copyright holder and licensing
contact. Do not treat a repository issue, chat message, or payment receipt by
itself as a signed commercial license.

Third-party dependencies and assets are not automatically included in an ArtFlow
commercial license. Their separate license notices and redistribution conditions
must be reviewed independently.
